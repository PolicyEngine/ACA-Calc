In Minnesota, New York, Oregon and DC, the calculator's results name the state's Basic Health Program, which covers adults aged 19 to 64 with incomes above Medicaid's limit up to 200% FPL (250% in New York until June 30, 2026), and say the results don't include it yet. Where the model gives no credit because the program covers the household, the explanation names the program as the reason, but only for members aged 19 to 64; members aged 65 or older are told that Medicare rules out the credit. The explanation of Medicaid's work requirement says it applies to the expansion group and that the estimate assumes no exemption, and three explanations now name their year.
