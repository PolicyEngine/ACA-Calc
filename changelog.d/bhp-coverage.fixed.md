The calculator API reports Basic Health Program eligibility (MinnesotaCare, New York's Essential Plan, OHP Bridge and the Healthy DC Plan), and the "Your household" cards and explanations name the program for the members it covers, in place of a Marketplace premium those members can't buy. With an answer from an API that doesn't report this coverage, the results instead name the state's program and say they don't include it. In New York, where the 2026 card has the Essential Plan and the 2027 card no program, the explanation says the 2026 card applies the 250% FPL limit to the whole year, though it fell to 200% on July 1, 2026. The full premium prices only members no program covers. The payment chart breaks where the model prices no plan, its credit matches the cards' to the dollar, and its % FPL rounds down as the cards' does. The data check compares each example's benchmark premium and credit as well as its payment, and requires every Overview household.
