Retell the Overview around 2027 premiums: how benchmark premiums rise from 2026 under current law, who pays the increase, and what H.R. 1834 would mean for four example households, from precomputed policyengine-us results. A new section charts the Pennsylvania example's marginal tax rate across incomes, with current law's cliff at 400% FPL and H.R. 1834's 8.5% phase-out (precompute_cliff_household.py now saves household net income and marginal rates). The Overview is now a single column, with each chart under its text, and the app uses PolicyEngine's colors and type throughout. The Overview opens with three key figures that count up, labels its lines directly (with a legend where they run too close), fades its charts in as they scroll into view, shows a section rail on wide screens, and marks the hovered income on each chart. A data check (scripts/check_data.mjs) runs before every build and fails it if the precomputed files disagree.
